About Brichaus Group

Brichaus Group, Inc. is an Encino-based real estate firm. Our main work is property management and HOA management, run under the direct supervision of a California licensed broker. Brichaus is pronounced “brick house.”

Brokerage & Advisory

Brichaus Group, Inc. is a California licensed real estate broker (DRE #02247407). Our main work is property management and HOA management. We also provide brokerage and advisory services on a limited basis, mainly for owners and associations we already work with. If you are thinking about buying, selling or repositioning a property, schedule a conversation and we will tell you plainly whether we are the right fit.

How we work

Boutique service, with an enterprise operating system behind it.

  • Advice is part of the job. Market conditions, asset strategy and operations come up in every engagement. You don't pay separately to hear what we think.
  • Systems carry the routine work. Automated owner reporting, portal access and a defined monthly calendar keep the routine on time, so the people you talk to have time to think.
  • You deal with the broker. Larry Jacobs supervises every account and stays personally involved with the owners and associations Brichaus manages.
  • Conditions are stated before engagement. If a property or association needs more than the published scope, you hear that before anyone signs.

Leadership

Larry founded Brichaus after 30 years in oil and gas at Shell, where his work included retail asset management and brokerage on the company's behalf. He has held a California broker's license for nearly 30 years, owns rental property himself, and has served twice on his own HOA board.
