Financial controls

Two controls do most of the work.

01

Operating and reserve funds kept separate

Assessments, operating spending and reserve contributions are recorded in separate funds, so the board sees what was collected, what was spent and what is set aside for capital work without untangling a combined ledger.

02

Every account reconciled to the bank, every month

Each operating and reserve account is reconciled against its bank statement monthly and delivered in the board's financial package with A/R aging, as Civil Code §5500 expects the board to review.

How an engagement starts

The condition is confirmed before anyone signs.

Published rates assume an association in ordinary condition: reconciled books with operating and reserve funds kept separate, delinquency in the ordinary range, and no active litigation or overdue inspection obligations. The first conversation is how we confirm that, and anything that differs is put in writing before engagement.

Engagements run on a twelve-month term with sixty-day termination either way. See what the rate assumes.
