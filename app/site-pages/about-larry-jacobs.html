Larry Jacobs

Founder, broker and designated officer of Brichaus Group, Inc. Larry supervises every Brichaus account and works directly with the owners and HOA boards the firm serves.

Schedule a Conversation

Background

Thirty years running real estate for a global operator.

Before founding Brichaus, Larry spent 30 years in oil and gas with Shell. His work there included retail asset management and real estate brokerage on the company's behalf. His last four years at Shell were in the Energy Transition business, working on solar, battery storage and virtual power plants.

He has held a California real estate broker's license for nearly 30 years, keeping it active with this work in mind. After retiring from Shell, he founded Brichaus Group in Encino.

Why it matters to clients

He has sat on both sides of the table.

  • As an owner. Larry owns rental property himself, so he manages client property against the standard he expects for his own.
  • As a board member. He has served twice on his own HOA board, and he stays personally involved in the associations Brichaus manages.
  • As a broker. Every Brichaus account runs under his direct supervision as the firm's designated broker.
